GTO Poker Coach Privacy Policy

Updated and effective November 23, 2026

Fujian Maoniu Technology Co., Ltd. operates this app for simulated poker practice and hand review. No real-name registration is required. There is no player-to-player wagering, chip top-up, cash-out or cash reward. Chips have no cash value. 简体中文

1. Records on your device

Learning records, hand results, statistics and settings are stored on your current device. There is no cross-device learning-record account sync. You can clear learning records in Practice review. This does not necessarily clear settings or previously displayed tips. Uninstalling may remove local records. Local records are controlled by you and are not subject to our server retention period below.

2. Online practice

Computer opponents and strategy references require a network connection. The training service receives the relevant hole cards, board, stacks, seats, player states, action history and random table identifiers to calculate actions and strategy references. Servers also receive necessary operational logs, including IP addresses, request times, endpoints and errors, to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. You do not need to provide your real name, phone number or login account.

3. Optional AI explanations

Before generating an explanation, we explain its data use and request your consent. Only after you choose to agree and generate do we send that hand for explanation. Declining does not prevent basic practice or review. When enabled, the service sends the hand, board, stacks, action history, chosen action and strategy reference to DeepSeek. Feedback email addresses are not added to these requests. DeepSeek is a third-party processor subject to its own privacy policy and terms; we do not promise that it never retains data or that processing is fully anonymous. AI text may be wrong and cannot guarantee results.

4. Voluntary feedback

You choose whether to send feedback by email. We receive the sending email address and content to reply and resolve issues. Email providers participate in transmission and storage. Do not send passwords, card details or unnecessary sensitive information. App Store reviews are voluntary and are not required to continue practicing.

5. Retention and protection

Developer-managed service logs, AI explanation records and feedback emails are retained for three years, then deleted or anonymized, except where law requires longer retention. Third-party services apply their own policies and terms; we do not promise deletion by third parties after three years. You control and can clear local learning records. Service connections use HTTPS, and we restrict data access and maintain security safeguards. Protect your device and avoid including unnecessary personal information in feedback.

6. Permissions and tracking

When available, overseas iOS membership uses optional Apple In-App Purchase subscriptions. We do not sell cash-redeemable chips. There is no advertising tracking, location, contacts, camera or recording feature. Playing local sound effects does not require microphone access. You can control permissions in system settings and disable sound effects in the app.

7. Your choices and rights

You can decline explanations, stop online practice or clear local learning records. Contact us to request access, a copy, correction or deletion of relevant personal information, or to withdraw consent. We verify and handle requests under applicable law. Where legal obligations prevent immediate deletion, we explain and restrict unrelated use. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing performed before it.

8. Age and policy changes

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and older, not children. Guardians can contact us if a child has provided information. We update this page when the policy changes and request consent separately where required for new data uses.

9. Privacy and support contact

Email hewen@maoniunet.com. Describe the relevant feature and necessary details without sending account passwords, identity documents or payment details.

10. Overseas membership and Apple purchases

Apple processes membership payments. We do not receive card numbers or Apple Account passwords. To verify purchases, renewals, refunds and restored purchases, and to manage allowances, the membership service stores a random user identifier, hashes of secure credentials, Apple transaction identifiers, products, purchase and expiry times, revocation status, table-restart records and cached AI explanations generated with your consent. The random membership identifier is not a real-name account and is not used for advertising tracking. Restoring purchases does not sync local learning records. Membership and explanation records follow the retention and protection rules above. Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple, and contact us about access or deletion, subject to legal financial-record retention obligations. Clearing local learning records does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete server membership records.

11. Scenarios and image sharing

Scenario drafts and local history are stored on your device. To request strategy, the membership service receives your hole cards, revealed board, player positions and states, stacks and action history. It stores scenario identifiers, analyzed nodes, strategy results and usage records for quotas, retries and caching, subject to the retention and protection rules above. Future preselected board cards stay on your device until revealed; opponent hole cards are not submitted. Review images are generated on your device and passed to your chosen app only when you share them. Complete reviews may include cards and actions; discussion mode can hide answers. Recipient platforms process shared content under their own policies.
