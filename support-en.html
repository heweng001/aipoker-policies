GTO Poker Coach · Support

Practice simulated Texas Hold’em with computer opponents and review your decisions. No real-name registration is required. Chips have no cash value. There is no player-to-player wagering, chip purchase, cash-out or cash prize.

Getting started

Tap Start/Continue Practice on launch, then wait for your turn at the six-player table, then fold, check, call or raise. View the strategy reference after your decision and tap Continue when shown. Open Settings for language and sound controls. Hand reviews and statistics are available from the table.

Scenarios and sharing

Open Review and choose New Scenario. Set a 2–6 player table, your hole cards, board, seat and starting stacks, then enter actions from preflop. Strategy references appear at your decisions. Undo and branch to explore alternatives. Scenarios do not affect practice scores. Free users receive three lifetime scenario credits; continuing the same scenario or viewing generated results does not spend another credit. Review tabs contain practice hands, new scenarios and scenario records. Export review images through system sharing, with an optional discussion view that hides answers. Restoring purchases does not sync local hands or scenarios.

Connection problems

Opponent actions and strategy services require internet access. Check your connection and use Retry Bots when available. If the issue continues, email the app version, device model, iOS version and the visible error message. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Optional membership

From version 1.0.6, both Apple subscription plans include unlimited practice restarts. Monthly members have unlimited heads-up scenarios. Annual members have unlimited 2–6 player scenarios and up to 300 successful AI text explanations per month. Monthly membership does not include AI explanations. Existing successful explanations are cached without another credit; different languages use separate credits. Free users may practice unlimited hands and receive 5 starter restarts, then 1 per day starting at the next 00:00 UTC after the starter credits are used. Daily credits do not roll over.

Restore or cancel

Restore Purchases is at the bottom of Settings. Use the Apple account associated with the purchase. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple account. Deleting local practice records or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. If verified benefits do not appear, retry on a stable connection and contact support without sending payment credentials.

Local records and AI explanations

Practice records are stored on this device and do not sync across devices. Uninstalling may remove them. AI explanations are optional and require consent to send the disclosed hand and strategy context to DeepSeek through our service. Declining preserves free practice and basic review. Model estimates and AI text may be wrong and do not guarantee winnings.
