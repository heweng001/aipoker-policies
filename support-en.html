GTO Poker Coach · Support

Practice simulated Texas Hold’em with computer opponents and review your decisions. No real-name registration is required. Chips have no cash value. There is no player-to-player wagering, chip purchase, cash-out or cash prize.

Getting started

Wait for your turn at the six-player table, then fold, check, call or raise. View the strategy reference after your decision and tap Continue when shown. Open Settings for language and sound controls. Hand reviews and statistics are available from the table.

Connection problems

Opponent actions and strategy services require internet access. Check your connection and use Retry Bots when available. If the issue continues, email the app version, device model, iOS version and the visible error message. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Optional membership

Both Apple subscription plans include 300 successful AI insights per month. Monthly members get 3 restarts per day; annual members get unlimited restarts. Daily credits reset at 00:00 UTC and do not roll over. Existing successful explanations are cached without another credit; different languages use separate credits. Free users may practice unlimited hands and receive 5 starter restarts, then 1 per day starting at the next 00:00 UTC after the starter credits are used. Daily credits do not roll over.

Restore or cancel

Restore Purchases is at the bottom of Settings. Use the Apple account associated with the purchase. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple account. Deleting local practice records or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. If verified benefits do not appear, retry on a stable connection and contact support without sending payment credentials.

Local records and AI explanations

Practice records are stored on this device and do not sync across devices. Uninstalling may remove them. AI explanations are optional and require consent to send the disclosed hand and strategy context to DeepSeek through our service. Declining preserves free practice and basic review. Model estimates and AI text may be wrong and do not guarantee winnings.
